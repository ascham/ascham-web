Ascham

Vocabulary practice from the texts you already teach.

Ascham, named after Roger Ascham, Tudor scholar and tutor to Elizabeth I.

Pronounced ASH-um /ˈæʃ.əm/, our chosen pronunciation.

What it does

  • Paste a text, or only the passage you are teaching.
  • Ascham picks the words worth teaching, each with the sentence it came from, word for word, and an estimated level, marked ~B2 because it is an estimate.
  • Tick the words you want, then print a gap-fill worksheet or a word list, or download cards for Anki.
  • Save your work as a project file on your own computer.

Try it free: open Ascham.

Your text stays with you

The text you paste never leaves your device.

Every byte ships from our own site: no third-party code, analytics or tracking.

No account is needed.

What we can and cannot see is on our Privacy page.

Free, and Pro

Free for as long as you use it: paste, review, print the word list, download a basic Anki deck, save projects and see a sample of the worksheet.

Pro is a one-time $15 for Ascham 1: the whole worksheet, printed, with the full text, your own questions and an answer key; cloze cards for Anki; your own deck tag and blocked words.
