# Reporting a vulnerability

Please report it privately, by email to security@ascham.app. To encrypt it,
use our OpenPGP key, [`public/security.asc`](public/security.asc),
fingerprint `9351 0FD3 9C07 490B 0FC7 A9AF 91FF D6D9 3D2E FB02`.

Include:

- what the problem is and what an attacker could do with it;
- the steps to show it, with a made-up text and made-up names;
- your browser and its version, and the page or commit you tested.

**Never send pasted text from a real class, students' work, or any student
data, and never send a licence key.** If the problem only shows with a
particular text, describe its shape (length, characters, layout) or write a
made-up text that does the same.

Please give us a reasonable time to fix it before you publish. We will say in
`CHANGELOG.md` when the fix is live, and credit you there if you want to be
named.
